# Storytelling Templates for Video Production

Structured beat formulas for different short-form video formats. Each template includes beat structure, timing distribution, fill-in-the-blank sentences, and TTS writing rules.

Use these templates during Step 2 (Write Script) of the video builder workflow.

---

## How to Use These Templates

1. **Match the template to the content goal**: what are you trying to achieve with this video?
2. **Fill in the blanks** with your specific topic, brand, or story
3. **Check timing**: multiply each beat's percentage by your target duration to get seconds per beat
4. **Apply TTS rules** to every sentence before sending to voiceover generation
5. **Adjust word count** based on style guide's target WPM and duration

---

## Template 1: Brand Documentary

**Best for**: Brand stories, company profiles, "how X became Y" narratives
**Typical duration**: 55-70 seconds
**Word count**: 150-190 words
**WPM**: ~170

### Beat Structure

| Beat | Name | % of Total | Purpose |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Hook** | 5% (~3s) | Bold claim or surprising fact that stops the scroll |
| 2 | **Origin** | 15% (~10s) | Where the story begins: the founding moment or problem |
| 3 | **Contrast** | 15% (~10s) | What made this different from everything else |
| 4 | **Reframe** | 15% (~10s) | The insight or philosophy that drove the approach |
| 5 | **Emotional Proof** | 15% (~10s) | Specific story or example that creates feeling |
| 6 | **Authority Proof** | 15% (~10s) | Numbers, scale, market position: the hard evidence |
| 7 | **Scale Proof** | 10% (~7s) | How big this became: global reach, revenue, impact |
| 8 | **Closer** | 10% (~6s) | Restate the thesis. Two-word or one-sentence closer. |

### Fill-in-the-Blank Script

```
HOOK:
"[Company/Brand] [did something seemingly impossible/counterintuitive]."
-- or --
"there's a reason [surprising fact about the company]."

ORIGIN:
"in [year], [founder name] [started/noticed/realized] [the origin story].
[one sentence about the early context or problem]."

CONTRAST:
"but [what everyone else was doing at the time].
[company] did the opposite -- [specific contrarian decision]."

REFRAME:
"[founder/company] understood something most people miss.
[the core philosophy in one sentence]."

EMOTIONAL PROOF:
"[specific story: a customer, a moment, a detail].
[why this mattered to real people]."

AUTHORITY PROOF:
"today [company] [operates/generates/serves] [specific number].
[second metric that reinforces scale]."

SCALE PROOF:
"[global reach metric] -- [countries, cities, customers].
[one line about cultural impact]."

CLOSER:
"[two-word closer that encapsulates the thesis]."
-- or --
"that's [what the company represents in one phrase]."
```

### Example (Yamato Transport style)

> there's a reason Japan has the best delivery system on earth.
> in 1919, a man named Yasuomi Kosuge started a small trucking company in Tokyo. Japan was rebuilding, and moving goods was slow and unreliable.
> but while other companies focused on commercial freight, Kosuge's son had a different idea. deliver packages directly to homes. everyone said it was impossible.
> they understood something most logistics companies miss. the last mile is not a cost center. it is the entire product.
> a Yamato driver will bow when handing you a package. they carry your delivery to your door in white gloves. every interaction is designed to feel personal.
> today Yamato handles over two billion parcels a year. that is five million deliveries every single day.
> they operate in every prefecture in Japan. there are more Yamato pickup points than there are convenience stores.
> logistics as hospitality.

### TTS Rules for This Template

- Use periods, not em dashes, between sentences. This template reads at a measured, documentary pace
- Spell out all numbers ("two billion" not "2B")
- No questions. The narrator is authoritative, not curious
- Short sentences (8-14 words) after longer ones create the documentary rhythm
- End the closer on a strong noun or noun phrase, never a verb

## Template 2: Proof Drop

**Best for**: Showing results, case studies, tool demos with outcomes
**Typical duration**: 30-45 seconds
**Word count**: 75-110 words
**WPM**: ~165

### Beat Structure

| Beat | Name | % of Total | Purpose |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Hook** | 10% (~4s) | State the result or transformation upfront |
| 2 | **Problem** | 20% (~8s) | What the viewer is struggling with |
| 3 | **Solution Demo** | 35% (~14s) | Show the tool/method step by step (max 3 steps) |
| 4 | **Result** | 20% (~8s) | Concrete outcome with numbers |
| 5 | **CTA** | 15% (~6s) | Comment, follow, or DM call to action |

### Fill-in-the-Blank Script

```
HOOK:
"I [built/made/generated/saved] [impressive result] [in timeframe]."
-- or --
"this [tool/method] just [changed/replaced/eliminated] [thing everyone uses]."

PROBLEM:
"most people [spend/waste/struggle with] [specific pain point].
[one sentence about why the old way is broken]."

SOLUTION DEMO:
"all you do is [step 1: open or go to the thing].
[step 2: the main action].
[step 3: the result appears]."

RESULT:
"[the specific outcome with a number].
[second metric or comparison that makes it tangible]."

CTA:
"comment [KEYWORD] and I'll [send you the link/DM you the prompt/share the template]."
-- or --
"follow for more [category]."
```

### TTS Rules for This Template

- Use "just" and "all you do" to minimize perceived difficulty
- Em dashes (`--`) between steps for punchy pauses
- ALL CAPS on the result number for emphasis ("saved TWENTY hours")
- Keep under 110 words. Proof drops are punchy, not narrative
- The CTA must include "I'll DM you" or "I'll send you". Personal beats impersonal

## Template 3: Contrarian Filter

**Best for**: Opinion content, "everyone is wrong about X," hot takes with substance
**Typical duration**: 35-50 seconds
**Word count**: 90-130 words
**WPM**: ~175

### Beat Structure

| Beat | Name | % of Total | Purpose |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Hot Take** | 10% (~4s) | Provocative statement that challenges conventional wisdom |
| 2 | **Why Most Are Wrong** | 25% (~10s) | Explain the common belief and why it fails |
| 3 | **The Real Answer** | 30% (~13s) | Your contrarian position with reasoning |
| 4 | **Evidence** | 20% (~8s) | Proof: numbers, examples, personal experience |
| 5 | **Reframe** | 15% (~6s) | Restate the take in a memorable way |

### Fill-in-the-Blank Script

```
HOT TAKE:
"[common advice/belief] is [wrong/backwards/costing you money]."
-- or --
"stop [doing the thing everyone does]. it [doesn't work/is outdated]."

WHY MOST ARE WRONG:
"everyone says [the conventional wisdom].
but [specific reason why this fails: data, logic, or counterexample].
[optional: I used to believe this too.]"

THE REAL ANSWER:
"the people who [get results/win/succeed] do [the opposite/this instead].
[explain the contrarian approach in 2-3 sentences].
[why this works: the underlying mechanism]."

EVIDENCE:
"[personal result: I did X and got Y].
[or: company Z switched to this and got Y result].
[specific number that proves the point]."

REFRAME:
"[restate the core idea in a quotable one-liner]."
-- or --
"[the old way] is [negative adjective]. [the new way] is [positive adjective]."
```

### TTS Rules for This Template

- Open with a confident, declarative tone, no hedging words ("maybe," "I think," "in my opinion")
- An em dash (`--`) before the evidence beat for dramatic weight
- Use "you" heavily in the first two beats (the viewer is the one being wrong)
- Switch to "I" in the evidence beat (you are the proof)
- The reframe should be quotable: short, punchy, memorable

## Template 4: Trend Jack

**Best for**: Reacting to news, new tool launches, industry shifts, viral moments
**Typical duration**: 30-45 seconds
**Word count**: 80-120 words
**WPM**: ~180

### Beat Structure

| Beat | Name | % of Total | Purpose |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Trending Event** | 15% (~5s) | Name the event/news that just happened |
| 2 | **Why It Matters** | 25% (~10s) | Connect the news to the viewer's life/work |
| 3 | **Your Angle** | 35% (~13s) | Your unique take or how to use this |
| 4 | **Takeaway** | 25% (~9s) | What to do about it + optional CTA |

### Fill-in-the-Blank Script

```
TRENDING EVENT:
"[major company/person] just [launched/announced/released] [specific thing]."
-- or --
"[thing] just changed and [nobody is talking about it / most people missed this]."

WHY IT MATTERS:
"this means [direct impact on the viewer].
[one sentence about the scale or significance].
[optional: why this is different from previous attempts]."

YOUR ANGLE:
"here's [what I'm doing with this / how to use this right now].
[step 1 or insight 1].
[step 2 or insight 2].
[optional: the non-obvious implication]."

TAKEAWAY:
"[one sentence summary of what to do].
[CTA: follow for updates / comment for the link / save this]."
```

### TTS Rules for This Template

- Lead with the entity name for authority: "Google just..." "OpenAI just..."
- Use "just" to create urgency, this happened NOW
- Keep sentences short and fast. Trend jacks are high energy
- Em dashes (`--`) for rapid transitions between thoughts
- ALL CAPS on the most surprising number or fact
- "Nobody is talking about this" / "most people missed this" are power hooks for this format

## Template 5: Listicle

**Best for**: Tool roundups, tips, "X things that Y," resource lists
**Typical duration**: 35-50 seconds
**Word count**: 90-130 words
**WPM**: ~175

### Beat Structure

| Beat | Name | % of Total | Purpose |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Hook Number** | 10% (~4s) | "X [things/tools/secrets] that [promise]" |
| 2 | **Item 1** | 20% (~8s) | First item: make it the most surprising |
| 3 | **Item 2** | 20% (~8s) | Second item: the most practical or useful |
| 4 | **Item 3** | 20% (~8s) | Third item: the crowd favorite or most relatable |
| 5 | **Synthesis** | 15% (~6s) | Why these matter together + what to do |
| 6 | **CTA** | 15% (~6s) | Follow, comment, save |

### Fill-in-the-Blank Script

```
HOOK NUMBER:
"[3/5/7] [tools/things/secrets/mistakes] that [will change X / most people don't know / I wish I knew sooner]."

ITEM 1:
"number one -- [name/description].
[one sentence about what it does and why it's surprising]."

ITEM 2:
"number two -- [name/description].
[one sentence about the practical benefit]."

ITEM 3:
"[and/finally] number three -- [name/description].
[one sentence about why this is the crowd favorite]."

SYNTHESIS:
"[one sentence tying them together: what they have in common, or how to use them together]."

CTA:
"save this for later."
-- or --
"comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [the full list / the links]."
-- or --
"follow for more [category]."
```

### TTS Rules for This Template

- Say the number explicitly: "number one," "number two," not just "first, second"
- Each item gets exactly 2 sentences, no more
- The first item should be the most scroll-stopping
- The last item should be the most universally useful
- Keep the total to 3-5 items. More than 5 loses attention in short-form
- An em dash (`--`) after each number word for a beat of anticipation

## Universal TTS Writing Rules

Apply these to every script regardless of template:

### Do

- Spell out numbers below 1000 ("three hundred" not "300")
- Use em dashes for punchy pauses between ideas, written `--` in these templates
- Use periods for full stops and natural breaths
- Use ALL CAPS for words that need vocal stress
- Keep sentences between 8-18 words
- End sentences on strong nouns, not prepositions
- Use active voice exclusively
- Front-load the most important word in each sentence

### Do Not

- Use rhetorical questions (TTS reads them with unnatural rising intonation) unless the style guide explicitly uses questions
- Use parenthetical asides (nested clauses sound robotic)
- Use semicolons (TTS handles them inconsistently)
- Use ellipses (creates awkward pauses)
- Use em dashes more than twice per paragraph
- Use words with ambiguous pronunciation without phoneme tags
- Exceed the style guide's target word count by more than 10%
- Use filler phrases ("it's worth noting," "interestingly enough," "as a matter of fact")

### Pronunciation Fixes (ElevenLabs)

For words the TTS mispronounces, use phoneme tags:
```
<phoneme alphabet="cmu-arpabet" ph="K L AW1 D">Claude</phoneme>
```

Common fixes:
- "AI": spell it out as "A.I." to avoid it reading as the word "ay"
- Brand names: test once and add phoneme tags if wrong
- Technical terms: add phoneme tags proactively

## Choosing a Template

| If the content is about... | Use |
|---------------------------|-----|
| A company, brand, or person's story | **Brand Documentary** |
| Showing a result, outcome, or demo | **Proof Drop** |
| Challenging conventional wisdom | **Contrarian Filter** |
| Reacting to news or a launch | **Trend Jack** |
| Listing tools, tips, or resources | **Listicle** |

When unsure, ask the user: "Is this video about a story, a result, an opinion, news, or a list?" That maps 1:1 to the templates.

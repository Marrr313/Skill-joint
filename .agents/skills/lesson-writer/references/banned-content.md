# Banned content

Check every lesson against this list before publishing. These words, phrases, and patterns read as machine-written no matter which voice profile is loaded.

## Banned words

delve, tapestry, landscape (as metaphor), realm, beacon, testament, multifaceted, treasure trove, nuanced, pivotal, robust, crucial, leverage (as a verb), foster, harness, illuminate, facilitate, bolster, underscore, embark, synergy, seamless, streamline, curated, comprehensive, transformative, revolutionary, cutting-edge, groundbreaking, innovative (without specifics), journey (as cliche), utilize, commence, moreover, furthermore, additionally, consequently, subsequently, showcasing, exemplifies, nestled, vibrant, profound, breathtaking, stunning, renowned, garnered, interplay, intricate, intricacies

## Banned phrases

- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "In an ever-evolving landscape..."
- "Let's dive in" / "Let's delve into..."
- "Welcome to [topic]..."
- "In this lesson, we will..."
- "By the end of this lesson, you will have learned..."
- "It is worth noting that..."
- "It is important to note that..."
- "In conclusion" / "In summary" / "In essence"
- "Unlock the potential of..."
- "Unleash the power of..."
- "Pave the way for..."
- "At the forefront of..."
- "serves as a testament"
- "stands as a reminder"
- "reflects broader trends"
- "marking the future" / "shaping the future"
- "setting the stage for"
- "I hope this helps"
- "Would you like me to..."
- "Great question!"
- "You're absolutely right!"

## Banned patterns

- Em dashes. Use commas, colons, or parentheses.
- Rule of three ("fast, flexible, and powerful")
- Negative parallelism ("It's not X, it's Y" / "Not because X. Because Y.")
- Vertical lists built from "**Term:** description" lines. Write prose instead.
- "The big idea" as a heading
- "Here is how it works" as the default heading in every lesson
- Uniform paragraph lengths across a whole lesson
- Every section following an identical internal format
- Title Case In Headings. Use sentence case.
- Generic positive closers ("The future looks bright")
- Exactly five bullet points in every list
- Three identically shaped sections in a row
- A closing paragraph that restates the lesson's point as a moral
- Vague allusion ("a popular tool," "experts say," "many people find")
- Physically performed emotion ("my stomach dropped," "my chest tightened")
